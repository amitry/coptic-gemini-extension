# ✉️ Email Announcement Draft: Coptic Gemini Assistant Launch

**Subject**: 🚀 Introducing Coptic Gemini Assistant: AI Clinical Decision Support for Unumed EHR

---

### **Dear Coptic Hospital Clinical Staff & Healthcare Team,**

We are excited to announce the official release and Web Store approval of **Coptic Gemini Assistant** (v7.0) — a powerful Chrome Extension created specifically for clinicians, medical officers, and nursing staff at Coptic Hospital in Lusaka, Zambia.

Designed to work directly inside our **Unumed EHR** system, the assistant brings point-of-care AI clinical decision support, automated guidelines alignment, and zero-click EHR form completion right to your workstation.

---

### 🌟 Key Capabilities & Clinical Benefits

1. **🇿🇲 Zambia STG & National Guidelines Alignment**:
   - Delivers real-time, evidence-based recommendations aligned with the **Zambia Standard Treatment Guidelines (STG)**, National Malaria Protocols, and Pediatric Dosing Tiers (e.g. weight-adjusted Artemether/Lumefantrine tiers).
   - Automatically suggests relevant **ICD-10 diagnostic codes** (e.g. `B50.9 Plasmodium falciparum malaria`).

2. **📊 Automated 5-Category EHR Patient Data Aggregator**:
   - Automatically intercepts and incorporates patient chart data in real-time as you review charts in Unumed:
     * 🩺 **Triage Vitals & Anthropometrics** (Weight, Temp, Pulse, SpO2, BMI)
     * 🧪 **Laboratory Reports** (Malaria RDT, Full Blood Count, Urine LAM)
     * 🖼️ **Imaging Reports** (Chest X-Ray, Abdominal Ultrasound)
     * 💊 **Active Medications & Ongoing Orders** (Paracetamol, IV Saline, ORS)
     * 📁 **External Referral Notes & Clinical History**

3. **🎯 1-Click "Smart Fill" EHR Form Auto-Populator**:
   - Eliminates repetitive manual typing. Click **`🎯 Smart Fill EHR Forms`** to automatically populate **Subjective (HPI)**, **Objective (Exam)**, **Assessment (Diagnosis)**, **Plan (Orders)**, and **ICD-10 Code** fields across Unumed form sections.

4. **💬 Interactive Point-of-Care Q&A Chat**:
   - Ask follow-up clinical questions directly inside the assistant window (e.g. *"What if RDT is negative?"*, *"Second-line ART options for pediatric patient?"*).

5. **🔒 Built-In Patient Data Privacy & Redaction**:
   - Automatically redacts sensitive identifiers (Zambian NRC numbers, patient full names, phone numbers) before processing.

### 🚀 How to Get Started in 2 Minutes

#### Step 1: Install from Chrome Web Store
1. Open Google Chrome on your hospital computer.
2. Visit the official extension page:  
   👉 **[Install Coptic Gemini Assistant from Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/bakdiggcjbafjnddaaljamnbbgajpimk)**
3. Click **"Add to Chrome"** and confirm installation.

#### Step 2: Sign In with your `@coptichospital.org` Account
1. Click the **Coptic Assistant icon** 🏥 in your Chrome extension toolbar (top-right of your browser).
2. Click **"Sign in with Google"** and select your official `@coptichospital.org` Google account.  
   *(No API keys or manual passwords required)*

#### Step 3: Start Using Inside Unumed EHR
1. Log in to Unumed EHR (`*.unumed.net`) and open any patient chart.
2. Look for the **`✨ Gemini Assist`** button next to clinical observation text areas, OR click the glowing **`✨ Coptic Gemini Assistant`** widget in the bottom-right corner of your screen.
