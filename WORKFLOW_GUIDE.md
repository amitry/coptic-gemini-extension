# 🩺 Optimal Clinical Workflow Guide for Doctors

**Coptic Gemini Assist (v3.2)** is designed to minimize charting time and provide point-of-care decision support without interrupting your clinical flow.

---

## ⚡ The 3-Step Ideal Workflow

### 1️⃣ Open the Patient Chart in Unumed
* **What happens**: Simply open the patient’s file in Unumed as you normally do.
* **Behind the scenes**: The extension automatically captures **Vitals** (Weight, Temp, BP, BMI), **Recent Labs** (FBC, Malaria RDT, Urine LAM), **Active Orders**, and **Demographics** in the background. No manual data entry is needed!

---

### 2️⃣ Capture Observations & Click ✨ Gemini Assist
* **Hands-Free Dictation**: Click **`🎙️ Dictate`** next to any note field and speak your clinical observations hands-free (e.g., *"3 year old male brought by mother with high fever 38.8°C, vomiting for 2 days, poor oral intake"*).
* **Click `✨ Gemini Assist`**:
  * **Structured SOAP Note**: Automatically organizes your input + chart vitals/labs into Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan sections.
  * **STG Dosing Calculator**: Automatically computes weight-tiered drug dosages (e.g., Artemether-Lumefantrine, Paracetamol, Amoxicillin) aligned with the Zambia Standard Treatment Guidelines.
  * **ICD-10 Diagnostic Chips**: Suggests billable ICD-10 codes (e.g., `B50.9 - Falciparum malaria`) with 1-click copy buttons.

---

### 3️⃣ Click 🎯 Smart Fill & Navigate Unumed
* **Click `🎯 Smart Fill EHR Forms`**:
  * Automatically populates Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan, and ICD-10 fields on your active screen.
* **Seamless Cross-Tab Auto-Fill**:
  * As you click between different Unumed tabs (e.g., from *Patient Overview* -> *Diagnoses* -> *Prescriptions/Orders*), the background extension observer automatically detects new fields and fills them in 1 click!
* **Saved Analysis Persistence**:
  * If you switch pages, your AI analysis is preserved! Click the floating widget (**`✨ Coptic Gemini Assistant (📜 Saved Analysis Available)`**) in the bottom-right corner anytime to re-open your active patient's recommendation or ask follow-up questions in the Q&A chat drawer.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for Maximum Efficiency

| Scenario | Best Feature to Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Busy Ward Rounds** | Use **`🎙️ Dictate`** to speak observations hands-free while walking between beds. |
| **Pediatric Dosing** | Refer to the **🧮 STG Dosing Calculator** card at the top of the AI modal for instant weight-based mg & tablet counts. |
| **Filling Diagnostic Codes** | Click any **`📋 ICD-10 Code Chip`** to copy the code to your clipboard instantly. |
| **Unsure About Guideline Steps** | Type follow-up questions in the **💬 Point-of-Care Q&A Chat** drawer (e.g., *"What if Malaria RDT is negative?"*). |
